UAE and WinUAE
==============

The AGA chipset core of AGA-PISTORM (core/ in the source, and the HDMI sound
filters in arm/aga_hdmi_filter.h) contains code from
UAE and WinUAE, the Amiga emulator - some copied, more translated into C - and
elsewhere follows their way of doing things:

  blitter     the fill step (copied); normal blits and the line mode
              (translated); the fill table; the cycles per word
  copper      the classic UAE copper (translated); the WAIT/SKIP compare
  playfield   the fetch tables, fetch modes, display window, delay offset,
              BSCAN2 modulos and dual playfield split (copied); colour
              writes, register handlers and HAM (translated); UAE's fetch
              and line model
  sprites     start/stop and the DMA cycle steps (copied); SPRES and
              VSTART/VSTOP (translated)
  collisions  the CLXDAT read (copied); the CLXCON masks (translated)
  custom      setclr and a few short lines
  disk        the DD track length and gap; the DSKBYTR bits
  audio       four interrupt and timing rules, rewritten; no code copied
              for those. The A500/A1200 filters for sound over HDMI are
              WinUAE's filter() and rc_calculate_a0(), translated into
              fixed point (arm/aga_hdmi_filter.h)

Each file's header says what it takes, and says that it was changed from the
UAE/WinUAE code for AGA-PISTORM in 2026. The timing was also checked against
WinUAE's DMA records. Much of it is the older UAE/WinUAE code as kept in
E-UAE, from before WinUAE's 2024-2025 rewrite of custom.cpp and drawing.cpp.
