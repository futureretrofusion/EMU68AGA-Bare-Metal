UAE / E-UAE / WinUAE attributions
================================
The AGA-PiStorm chipset source in machine/core/src includes and
derives routines from UAE, E-UAE and WinUAE. The specific copied,
translated or inspired routines, original source files and
copyright years are detailed in the original per-file headers
in machine/core/src. Those file-level notices are preserved.

Original authors and contributors named in the source include:
Bernd Schmidt (UAE); Alessandro Bissacco; Toni Wilen
(WinUAE), and other UAE/E-UAE/WinUAE contributors.

This summary was reconstructed from source-level notices and is
not asserted to reproduce any missing original notice verbatim.
